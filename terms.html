Terms of Service

Last updated: January 2026

1. Scope of Services

Luyanda Buthelezi Physiotherapy provides professional physiotherapy services including but not limited to musculoskeletal rehabilitation, post-surgical recovery, sports injury management, chronic pain treatment and paediatric physiotherapy. All services are delivered by a registered physiotherapist in accordance with HPCSA standards of practice.

2. Appointment Responsibilities

Patients are responsible for arriving on time for scheduled appointments. If you are more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may need to be rescheduled. Please ensure you have completed any required pre-appointment paperwork before your session.

3. Patient Responsibilities

Patients are expected to provide accurate and complete medical information, follow the treatment plan agreed upon with the physiotherapist, and communicate any changes in their condition. Failure to comply with the treatment plan may affect outcomes and is not the responsibility of the practice.

4. Cancellation Policy

We require at least 24 hours' notice for appointment cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or missed appointments without notice may incur a cancellation fee. Repeated no-shows may result in the practice being unable to offer future appointments.

5. Medical Disclaimer

The information provided on this website, including blog content and service descriptions, is for general informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Patients should consult directly with the physiotherapist for personalised diagnosis and treatment recommendations.

6. Limitation of Liability

Luyanda Buthelezi Physiotherapy takes all reasonable care in delivering professional services. However, no healthcare provider can guarantee specific outcomes. The practice is not liable for any adverse effects resulting from a patient's failure to follow prescribed treatment plans or provide accurate medical information.

7. Privacy Reference

Your personal and medical information is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable South African data protection legislation, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
