Privacy Policy & Protection of Personal Information (POPIA)

Effective date: January 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Luyanda Buthelezi Physiotherapy. We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and handling your personal and medical information securely and confidentially, in full compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa and HPCSA statutory guidelines.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process information necessary to provide clinical physiotherapy care and administrative services, including:

  • Contact details (Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address, Physical Address)
  • Demographic data (Date of birth, ID number)
  • Medical information (Medical history, referring doctor notes, rehabilitation records, treatment plans)
  • Medical aid details (Scheme name, membership number, dependent code)
  • Communications sent via our contact and appointment request forms

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Clinical evaluation, physiotherapy diagnosis, and structured treatment planning
  • Scheduling, appointment confirmation, and practice communication via WhatsApp, SMS, or email
  • Medical aid billing, claims processing, and Prescribed Minimum Benefits (PMB) submissions
  • Compliance with South African healthcare regulations and statutory clinical recordkeeping requirements

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement rigorous technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal and medical records against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. All electronic records are securely encrypted and stored in compliance with healthcare data protection standards.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is only disclosed with your explicit consent or where legally mandated:

  • With medical aid schemes for claims processing
  • With referring medical doctors, orthopaedic surgeons, or allied healthcare providers involved in your treatment
  • Where required by South African law or lawful judicial process

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the legal right to:

  • Request access to the personal records we hold regarding your care
  • Request the correction or update of inaccurate personal data
  • Object to the processing of your personal information where applicable
  • Lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator if you believe your data rights have been infringed

7. Contact Our Information Officer

For questions or requests regarding your personal data and privacy rights, please reach out to:
