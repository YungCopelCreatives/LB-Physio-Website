Initial Assessment

Your journey begins with a thorough clinical assessment. We evaluate your medical history, current symptoms, functional limitations and lifestyle factors to build a complete picture of your condition.

  • Comprehensive medical history review
  • Functional movement assessment
  • Pain evaluation and measurement
  • Lifestyle and activity analysis
  • Goal setting with the patient

Clinical Diagnosis

Using evidence-based assessment techniques, we identify the root cause of your condition — not just the symptoms. This ensures targeted, effective treatment from the outset.

  • Evidence-based diagnostic techniques
  • Root cause identification
  • Risk factor assessment
  • Differential diagnosis consideration
  • Collaborative discussion with the patient

Personalised Treatment Plan

We design a tailored treatment plan that combines manual therapy, exercise prescription, patient education and lifestyle modifications — all aligned with your recovery goals.

  • Manual therapy techniques
  • Exercise prescription and programming
  • Patient education and self-management strategies
  • Lifestyle modification recommendations
  • Clear milestones and timelines

Rehabilitation & Recovery

Through hands-on treatment and guided exercise, we work with you to restore strength, mobility and function. Each session builds progressively toward your goals.

  • Progressive loading and strengthening
  • Mobility and flexibility work
  • Neuromuscular re-education
  • Functional task training
  • Gradual return to activity

Progress Monitoring

We continuously assess your progress, adjust your treatment plan as needed and ensure you are meeting key recovery milestones at every stage.

  • Regular progress assessments
  • Treatment plan adjustments
  • Outcome measurement tools
  • Communication with referring practitioners
  • Home exercise program updates

Return to Function / Sport

Our ultimate goal is your full return to the activities you love. We provide return-to-work and return-to-sport protocols to ensure a safe, confident transition.

  • Functional capacity testing
  • Sport-specific rehabilitation
  • Return-to-work planning
  • Self-management education
  • Maintenance and prevention strategies

Disclaimer: Treatment plans, recovery timelines and outcomes vary depending on injury severity, medical history, patient compliance and individual response to treatment. The information provided is for general guidance only and does not constitute medical advice.
