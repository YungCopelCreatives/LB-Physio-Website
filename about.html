About Luyanda Buthelezi

My name is Luyanda Buthelezi, a qualified Physiotherapist and Wits University graduate. Born and raised in Ladysmith, KZN, I am deeply committed to delivering comprehensive, patient-centered care.

Beyond the clinical setting, I am passionate about sports physiotherapy, general rehabilitation, and addressing the interplay between physical recovery and mental health.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every interaction and treatment at LB Physiotherapy.

Humility

We approach every patient with openness and a willingness to listen, learn and grow alongside them.

Genuine Interactions

Every conversation and treatment is built on authenticity, sincerity and real human connection.

Transparency

We communicate openly about diagnoses, treatment plans and expected outcomes so patients feel informed and empowered.

Honesty

We provide truthful, straightforward guidance so patients can make confident decisions about their care.

Integrity

We uphold the highest ethical standards in every aspect of our practice, from treatment to communication.

Patience

Recovery takes time. We walk alongside our patients with patience, ensuring no step is rushed or overlooked.

Compassion

We treat every patient with empathy, understanding and genuine care throughout their recovery journey.

Supportive

We provide consistent encouragement and guidance, ensuring patients feel supported at every stage of their healing.
