Spinal Mobility & Chronic Pain Relief

Clinical Case Overview

  • Condition: Lumbar Spine Disc Derangement & Sciatica
  • Discipline: Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation
  • Patient: Sandile M. (Office Professional)

Rehabilitation Protocol & Outcome

Tailored multimodal program incorporating joint mobilization, neurodynamic glides, core stabilization exercises, and ergonomic workstation restructuring. Achieved 100% pain reduction and full spinal mobility within 6 weeks.

Post-Surgical Ankle Ligament Rehab

Clinical Case Overview

  • Condition: Grade III ATFL Tear & Post-Op Ankle Reconstruction
  • Discipline: Sports Injury & Return-to-Play
  • Patient: Lusanda D. (Wanderers Chiefs Rugby Player)

Rehabilitation Protocol & Outcome

Phased sports-specific rehabilitation focused on proprioception, plyometrics, change-of-direction agility, and matchday load monitoring. Successfully returned to full rugby competition at peak performance without re-injury.

Scapular Dyskinesis & Shoulder Rehab

Clinical Case Overview

  • Condition: Scapular Dyskinesis & Cervicogenic Headaches
  • Discipline: Postural & Myofascial Therapy
  • Patient: Ntando M. (Athlete)

Rehabilitation Protocol & Outcome

Deep myofascial release, serratus anterior and lower trapezius neuromuscular re-education, and cervical spine manual therapy completely resolved neck discomfort and restored full shoulder range of motion.

Post-Surgical Functional Independence

Clinical Case Overview

  • Discipline: Post-Operative Rehabilitation
  • Patient: Wendy K.

Rehabilitation Protocol & Outcome

Graduated progressive resistance training and neuromuscular reactivation enabled the patient to walk unassisted and regain total independent daily functioning after 7 months of immobility.

Chronic Cervicothoracic Pain Management

Clinical Case Overview

  • Condition: Chronic Upper & Lower Back Spasm
  • Discipline: Chronic Pain Management
  • Patient: Mandisa J.

Rehabilitation Protocol & Outcome

Targeted manual therapy, dry needling, postural realignment exercises, and patient education eliminated dependence on pain medications and restored active lifestyle activities.

Total Knee Replacement (TKR) Recovery

Clinical Case Overview

  • Condition: Post-Operative Total Knee Arthroplasty
  • Discipline: Joint Replacement Rehabilitation
  • Patient: Thabiso K.

Rehabilitation Protocol & Outcome

Structured early mobilization, quadriceps reactivation, gait retraining, and swelling control achieved 120° knee flexion and safe stair navigation within 8 weeks post-surgery.

Clinical Note & Disclaimer: Case study outcomes reflect individual patient results. Recovery timelines and functional improvements vary depending on individual clinical presentation, injury chronicity, surgical procedure, and adherence to prescribed home rehabilitation protocols.
